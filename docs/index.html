About

I’m the co-founder and CTO of M11 Labs, building data and trust infrastructure for agentic commerce. I’m interested in how agents understand products, evaluate evidence, and make decisions.

My work spans multilingual AI, data provenance, and autonomous agents. I’ve contributed to Cohere’s Aya initiative and co-authored research on data consent and transparency. I previously chaired the AI Association of Lithuania.

Earlier work & background +

I’ve built my career at the intersection of AI, data, and business. After studying finance at ISM University of Management and Economics, I started in data analytics at INVL Asset Management, then worked alongside IBM’s data scientists on applied AI and forecasting.

In Zurich, I built and led the data analytics and AI function at Sunrise UPC. That experience shaped my focus: turning ambitious technical ideas into systems people can use.

As Chairperson of the AI Association of Lithuania, I brought researchers, founders, companies, and universities into the same conversation. I also co-presented AI_biblioteka, bringing AI to a new generation of students.

My ambition now is to help shape AI that works across languages, respects its data sources, and earns trust through the decisions it makes.
