About

I’m the co-founder and CTO of M11 Labs, building data and trust infrastructure for agentic commerce. I’m interested in how agents understand products, evaluate evidence, and make decisions.

My work spans multilingual AI, data provenance, and autonomous agents. I’ve contributed to Cohere’s Aya initiative and co-authored research on data consent and transparency. I previously chaired the AI Association of Lithuania.

Earlier work & background +

I studied finance at ISM University of Management and Economics and began my career in data analytics at INVL Asset Management. I then joined IBM, working alongside data scientists on applied AI and forecasting.

Later, in Zurich, I built and led a data analytics and AI function at Sunrise UPC.

As Chairperson of the AI Association of Lithuania, I helped connect researchers, founders, companies, and universities. I also co-presented AI_biblioteka, an educational series about AI for students.
